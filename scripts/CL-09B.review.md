## Plot accuracy notes
- Lear divides Britain by a love-test; Goneril & Regan flatter and are rewarded; Cordelia says "nothing"/loves "according to my bond", is disinherited; Kent banished for defending her; King of France marries dowerless Cordelia: correct.
- Mirror-plot: Gloucester, sons Edgar (legitimate) & Edmund (bastard); Edmund's villain speech; forged letter frames Edgar; Edgar flees as "Poor Tom": correct.
- Lear keeps title + 100 knights, lives with Goneril; she demands he halve them; he curses her, goes to Regan (worse); the sisters haggle the knights down to none ("What need one?"); Lear's "reason not the need": correct.
- Lear rushes into the storm; daughters bolt the doors; rages on the heath, goes mad; with the Fool (truth-teller, who vanishes mid-play unexplained) and disguised Kent; the hovel; Poor Tom; "unaccommodated man"/tries to strip; pity for "poor naked wretches"; "more sinn'd against than sinning": correct.
- Gloucester secretly aids Lear; Edmund betrays him; Cornwall gouges out both his eyes on stage; Regan reveals Edmund's betrayal; a servant mortally wounds Cornwall; Gloucester cast out: correct.
- Blinded Gloucester led (unknowing) by disguised Edgar; "as flies to wanton boys"; the Dover-cliff fake suicide; Edgar convinces him the gods preserved him ("ripeness is all"): correct.
- Cordelia returns with a French army; the reunion (Lear kneels, "foolish fond old man"; Cordelia "no cause"): correct.
- Goneril & Regan both lust for Edmund; Goneril poisons Regan then kills herself; British forces beat the French; Lear & Cordelia captured; Edmund's secret order to hang Cordelia; Edgar reveals himself, mortally wounds Edmund; dying Edmund repents too late; Cordelia hanged; Lear enters with her body, dies of grief; Gloucester died off-stage (heart "burst smilingly" at Edgar's reveal); survivors Edgar/Kent/Albany; Kent hints he'll follow Lear; Edgar's closing lines: correct.

- The Fool's unexplained disappearance noted as the famous crux it is.
