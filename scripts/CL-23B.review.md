## Plot accuracy notes
- Frame: Marlow narrates aboard the yawl Nellie on the Thames at dusk; "this also has been one of the dark places of the earth"; the Romans; the river like a snake; the "whited sepulchre" city; the two knitting women; the doctor; the aunt's "emissary of light"; the predecessor killed over hens: correct.
- Coast voyage; the French man-of-war shelling the bush ("a touch of insanity"): correct.
- Outer Station: senseless blasting, the chain-gang, the grove of death (dying labourers, the white worsted thread, the biscuit), the immaculate accountant; first mention of Kurtz: correct.
- 200-mile trek to Central Station; the sunk steamer & long delay; the hollow Manager (survives by health, inspires uneasiness); the "pilgrims" with staves worshipping ivory; the brick-maker who makes no bricks; the fire; Kurtz's legend (most ivory, a genius): correct.
- Upriver: "travelling back to the earliest beginnings of the world"; the brooding indifferent jungle; the superstitious fireman; the restrained cannibal crew & the hippo-meat; Marlow's uneasy kinship with the "wildness": correct.
- The fog; the attack from the banks; the helmsman speared and killed; body thrown overboard (jealousy re: cannibals); Kurtz as "a voice": correct. (Attack was on Kurtz's orders to prevent his removal — per the Russian: correct.)
- Inner Station: the harlequin Russian disciple ("enlarged my mind"); the heads on the stakes; Kurtz as unrestrained god/monster, raids, "unspeakable rites"; the barbaric African consort vs the Intended; Kurtz's eloquent civilising report + the "Exterminate all the brutes!" postscript: correct.
- Kurtz brought out a dying skeleton (the head & the voice); the Manager's "unsound method" (= profit/reputation, not morality); Kurtz crawls back toward the fires/drums, Marlow retrieves him by appealing to ambition/vanity; the "hollow at the core" diagnosis: correct.
- Retreat downriver; Kurtz's final clarity & "The horror! The horror!"; the servant's "Mistah Kurtz—he dead"; buried in a muddy hole; Marlow nearly dies; reads Kurtz's cry as a "moral victory": correct.
- Return to the "sepulchral city"; contempt for the oblivious Europeans; the Intended in perpetual mourning, her absolute faith; she begs for his last words; Marlow — who hates lies — lies that Kurtz said her name; "the heavens do not fall": correct. Closing frame image: the Thames leading "into the heart of an immense darkness": correct.
