## Plot accuracy (keyed to the play)
- Athens: Theseus's wedding; Egeus demands Hermia marry Demetrius; the law (marry / die / nunnery);
  Hermia & Lysander plan to elope; Hermia tells Helena; Helena loves Demetrius and betrays the plan. ✓
- The mechanicals (Quince, Bottom) plan to rehearse Pyramus and Thisbe in the wood. ✓
- The fairies: Oberon & Titania quarrel over the changeling boy; nature disordered; the love-flower;
  Oberon overhears Demetrius spurn Helena and sends Puck to anoint "the Athenian". ✓
- Puck anoints the wrong man (Lysander); Lysander wakes loving Helena; Oberon anoints Demetrius too;
  both men now love Helena; the four-way quarrel; "Lord, what fools these mortals be"; Puck fogs the
  men apart and cures Lysander. ✓
- Puck gives Bottom an ass's head; Titania wakes and dotes on him; Oberon takes the boy, pities and
  releases Titania; they reconcile; nature heals; Bottom un-assed. ✓
- Morning: Theseus finds the lovers (now correctly paired — Lysander/Hermia, Demetrius/Helena, the
  latter still under the flower), overrules Egeus; triple wedding; Bottom's "most rare vision"; the
  mechanicals' gloriously bad Pyramus and Thisbe; the fairies bless the house; Puck's epilogue. ✓

## Judgment calls
- Structured in five movements following the three interwoven plots; the braid kept explicit so the
  three groups (lovers / fairies / mechanicals) never blur.
- Notes that Demetrius is left permanently under the love-charm — a genuine, oft-discussed detail, not
  an error.
- Pyramus & Thisbe framed as the comic dark-mirror of the main plot (standard reading).
- End-screen points to CL-01B.
