## Plot accuracy notes

- Ball at Meryton; Darcy's "tolerable, but not handsome enough to tempt me" snub of Elizabeth: correct (paraphrased on-screen, quote is accurate to the text's sense; exact text "She is tolerable; but not handsome enough to tempt me").
- Jane sent on horseback → rain → ill at Netherfield; Elizabeth walks ~3 miles, muddy petticoats; Miss Bingley's mockery vs Darcy's admiration: correct.
- Mr Collins proposes to Elizabeth (refused), then Charlotte Lucas (age 27, accepts for security): correct.
- Wickham's false tale (denied the living by Darcy out of spite): correct as his claim; Darcy's letter later gives the true version (Wickham took £3,000 in lieu, squandered it, then attempted to elope with 15-yr-old Georgiana for her £30,000): correct.
- Darcy's first proposal at Hunsford (parsonage near Rosings / Lady Catherine); Elizabeth's furious refusal citing Jane, Wickham, and his arrogance ("last man in the world"): correct.
- The letter as the hinge; Elizabeth's "till this moment I never knew myself" reckoning: correct (line ref in text; sense preserved, not quoted on a card).
- Pemberley visit (Derbyshire), housekeeper Mrs Reynolds's praise, Darcy appears transformed, civil to the Gardiners (tradesman uncle): correct.
- Lydia elopes with Wickham from Brighton; threat to all sisters' reputations: correct.
- Darcy secretly finds them, pays Wickham's debts, buys a commission, forces the marriage; motivated by love + atonement for his earlier silence: correct.
- Bingley returns, engaged to Jane; Lady Catherine's confrontation at Longbourn backfires, giving Darcy hope: correct.
- Second proposal on a walk; both humbled/changed; double marriage; Elizabeth mistress of Pemberley; Mr Bennet's blessing: correct.
