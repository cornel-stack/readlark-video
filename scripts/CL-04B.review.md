## Judgment calls
- Structured in five movements; Iago foregrounded as the engine; his motivelessness ("motive-hunting
  of a motiveless malignity", Coleridge) named directly.
- The racism Othello faces, and his internalised outsider-insecurity as Iago's lever, addressed in the
  marriage scenes and the close — the standard modern reading, without over-claiming.
- Art uses Chassériau/Cabanel/Delacroix Othello paintings and a dignified portrait tradition.
