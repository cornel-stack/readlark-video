## Plot accuracy notes
- Oliver born in a workhouse, mother dies nameless; parish orphan under beadle Mr Bumble; baby farm (Mrs Mann) then workhouse; deliberate near-starvation under the board; boys draw lots; Oliver asks for more: correct.
- Branded a troublemaker; nearly sold to a chimney-sweep (Gamfield); apprenticed to undertaker Sowerberry; bullied by Noah Claypole; Noah insults his dead mother, Oliver fights him, is beaten/locked up; runs away, walks ~70 miles (7 days) to London: correct.
- Meets the Artful Dodger (Jack Dawkins); led to Fagin's den of boy thieves; the pocket-picking "game"; Oliver's innocence: correct.
- First outing with Dodger & Charley Bates; they pick Mr Brownlow's pocket; "Stop thief!"; Oliver wrongly caught; magistrate Mr Fang; bookseller's witness clears him; Brownlow takes the feverish Oliver home (Mrs Bedwin); the portrait resembling Oliver: correct.
- Oliver sent on an errand; Nancy & Sikes recapture him (Nancy poses as his sister); Brownlow left believing Oliver a thief; Monks appears, plotting with Fagin to corrupt (not just rob) Oliver: correct.
- Sikes's Chertsey burglary; Oliver forced through the window at gunpoint, resolves to warn the house, is shot, abandoned in a ditch; crawls to the robbed house — Mrs Maylie & niece Rose take him in (Dr Losberne); recovers in the country: correct.
- Nancy — raised by Fagin, devoted to abusive Sikes, conscience awakened; overhears Monks & Fagin (incl. the destroyed locket/ring proof); secretly meets Rose (and later Brownlow) to reveal the plot; refuses rescue / to betray the gang: correct.
- Noah Claypole (now in the gang) spies for Fagin; Fagin tells Sikes Nancy betrayed them; Sikes murders Nancy (she holds up Rose's handkerchief, prays); Sikes haunted by her eyes, hunted; dies accidentally hanging himself fleeing the mob at Jacob's Island; his dog dies: correct.
- Monks = Edward Leeford, Oliver's half-brother; father's will (inheritance conditional on Oliver staying untainted); Monks paid Fagin to criminalize Oliver; locket/ring (via the workhouse nurse → Mr & Mrs Bumble → Monks → river): correct. Rose Maylie = Agnes's sister = Oliver's aunt: correct.
- Dodger transported; Fagin tried, condemned; "Fagin's Last Night Alive" in the Newgate condemned cell; Oliver & Brownlow visit; Bumble ruined; Brownlow adopts Oliver; inheritance restored: correct. Monks given a share, squanders it, dies in prison: correct.
