- Backstory: Eteocles & Polynices kill each other; Creon takes the throne — prologue. ✓
- Creon's edict: Eteocles honoured, Polynices left unburied on pain of death — prologue/first episode. ✓
- Antigone resolves to bury him; Ismene refuses; they part estranged — prologue. ✓
- Ode to Man (first stasimon). ✓
- Sentry reports the symbolic burial; Creon assumes bribery; Antigone caught re-burying in daylight. ✓
- Antigone's defence (unwritten laws of heaven); Creon condemns her; Ismene tries to share the blame. ✓
- Haemon's plea (the bending tree); the quarrel; Creon decrees the walled-up cave. ✓
- Ode to Love (Eros, 3rd stasimon); Antigone's lament en route to the tomb. ✓
- Teiresias's warning and prophecy; Creon relents too late. ✓
- Messenger: Polynices buried; Antigone hanged; Haemon's suicide clinging to her; Eurydice's suicide
  and curse; Creon left alive and broken; closing choral moral on late wisdom. ✓
