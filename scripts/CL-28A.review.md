## Judgment calls
- Movements, not 24 books — matches the retelling's frame.
- Makes explicit that the poem is not the whole war (no wooden horse, no fall of Troy) and ends on a
  funeral — a point students routinely get wrong.
- Companion to CL-28B; end-screens cross-link both ways.
