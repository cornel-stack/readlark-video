## Plot accuracy (keyed to the play)
- The ancient Montague/Capulet feud; the opening brawl; the Prince's death-penalty decree. ✓
- Romeo lovesick for Rosaline; Mercutio & Benvolio; the Capulet ball; Romeo meets Juliet (the shared
  sonnet, first kiss); both learn the other's forbidden name. ✓
- The balcony scene; Juliet on names; the vows; she proposes marriage. ✓
- Friar Laurence marries them secretly (hoping to end the feud); the Nurse as go-between. ✓
- Tybalt kills Mercutio under Romeo's arm; Romeo kills Tybalt; "fortune's fool"; the Prince banishes
  Romeo; the wedding night and dawn parting; Capulet forces the Paris match; the Nurse's betrayal. ✓
- The Friar's sleeping-potion plan; Juliet's terror, then she drinks it; she is entombed; the letter

- The tomb: Romeo kills Paris; drinks poison moments before Juliet wakes; she wakes, refuses to flee,
  stabs herself; the Friar's account; Montague & Capulet reconcile over the bodies; the Prince's close. ✓

## Judgment calls
- Structured in six movements over the play's ~four compressed days.
- Frames the tragedy as caused by the feud + haste + impulsiveness together (not pure "fate"), and
  places blame on the adult world — a standard, well-supported reading.
- "wherefore = why" glossed on the card to prevent the common misreading.
- End-screen points to CL-01B.
