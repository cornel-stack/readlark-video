## Judgment calls / things for you to decide

1. **One quote is relocated in running order.** "I was benevolent and good; misery
   made me a fiend" (scene `v2-fiend`) is spoken verbatim by the creature in the
   **frame confrontation** (Vol II, Ch II), i.e. *before* he narrates his tale. In
   the retelling I place it *after* the cottage-rejection beat, as the thematic
   hinge where the innocent turns. The quote is verbatim and correctly cited to Vol
   II, Ch II; only its position in the *retelling's* sequence is dramatised. Flagging
   in case you'd rather it sit exactly where the book places it.
2. **Condensed quotes.** `close-victor-quote` and `close-fallen` use ellipses to
   stitch a longer passage into a card-length line. Every fragment is verbatim and in
   original order; nothing is reworded. If you prefer un-condensed quotes on the
   card, I can split them across two cards each.
3. **Edition / chapter numbering.** 1818 citations (see CL-11A review, point 1). Say
   the word if you want 1831 equivalents added.
4. **Safie "Arabian."** The creature calls her "the Arabian" (3554); her father is a
   Turkish merchant, her late mother a Christian Arab. I used "Arabian," matching the
   creature's own word. Flagging because some editions/notes describe her as Turkish.
5. **"Sorrows of Werter."** Named in the retelling as "a sorrowful novel about a
   heartbroken young man" rather than by title, to keep narration flowing for a
   16-year-old. It is Goethe's *The Sorrows of Young Werther*. Tell me if you'd
   rather name it.
6. **Voice** is a placeholder (`en-GB-RyanNeural`) pending Checkpoint 5.
