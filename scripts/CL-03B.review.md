## Plot accuracy (keyed to the play)
- Elsinore; old king dead; Claudius married Gertrude and took the throne; grieving Hamlet; the ghost
  on the battlements. ✓
- The ghost reveals Claudius's murder (poison in the ear), commands revenge, says spare Gertrude;
  Hamlet swears, then hesitates; his prior despair and disgust at the remarriage. ✓
- The feigned "antic disposition"; Rosencrantz & Guildenstern spy; Polonius & Ophelia; the nunnery
  scene; "To be or not to be"; the players and "The Mousetrap" catching Claudius's guilt. ✓
- Hamlet spares the praying Claudius (to damn his soul later); the closet scene; stabs Polonius through
  the arras; the ghost's second visit (only Hamlet sees it); Claudius sends Hamlet to England to die. ✓

  Yorick's skull; Laertes' thirst for revenge. ✓

  cut; Laertes confesses; Hamlet kills Claudius; "the rest is silence"; Horatio spared; Fortinbras. ✓

## Judgment calls
- Structured in five movements; foregrounds the central "delay" question and the three-avengers
  parallel (Hamlet / Laertes / Fortinbras) — standard scholarship.
- The ghost's ambiguity (purgatorial spirit vs demon) and Gertrude's uncertain complicity flagged,
  not resolved — as the text leaves them.
