## Plot accuracy notes
- Viola shipwrecked in Illyria, believes twin Sebastian drowned; disguises as the youth "Cesario", enters Duke Orsino's service: correct.
- Orsino lovesick for Countess Olivia (self-indulgent, in love with love); Olivia in mourning (dead father & brother), vowed 7 years' seclusion, rejects him: correct.
- Orsino sends Cesario to woo Olivia; Viola secretly loves Orsino; Olivia falls for Cesario — the triangle (Orsino→Olivia→Cesario/Viola→Orsino): correct. The "willow cabin / halloo your name to the hills" wooing speech and the veiled "sister who never told her love" speech: correct (paraphrased in narration).
- Subplot household: Sir Toby Belch (drunk uncle), Sir Andrew Aguecheek (foolish, being fleeced), Maria (clever waiting-woman), Feste (the clown/fool), Malvolio (puritanical steward); "cakes and ale" clash: correct.
- Maria forges a letter in Olivia's hand; Malvolio gulled into believing Olivia loves him; the box-tree eavesdropping; ordered to wear yellow cross-gartered stockings, smile, be rude to Toby — all things Olivia detests: correct.
- Malvolio appears thus to grieving Olivia; thought mad; bound in a dark room; Feste as "Sir Topas" torments him (dark room = "light"): correct.
- Sebastian alive, saved by devoted Antonio (who risks arrest to follow him); identical twins dressed alike → mistaken-identity chaos; Sir Andrew fights "Cesario" and gets Sebastian (thrashed); Antonio arrested, feels betrayed by "Sebastian" (really Viola); Olivia mistakes Sebastian for Cesario: correct.
- Olivia courts & marries Sebastian (on the spot, before a priest); he's bewildered but delighted: correct.
- Final scene: all converge; Olivia claims Cesario as husband; Orsino feels betrayed; Sebastian enters — twins face to face; reunion (questions re: father/mole); Viola reveals she is a woman: correct.
- Resolution: Olivia wed to Sebastian; Orsino, learning Cesario is a woman who loves him, weds Viola; Toby weds Maria; Malvolio released, "notoriously abused", storms off vowing "I'll be revenged on the whole pack of you"; Feste's closing song ("When that I was and a little tiny boy… the rain it raineth every day"): correct.
