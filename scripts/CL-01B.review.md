## Judgment calls
- "I 'gin to be aweary of the sun" (5.5) is paraphrased in `a5-birnam`, not quoted.
- The context scene's background (written c.1606 for James I; the "Scottish play"
  superstition; shortest tragedy) is standard non-plot framing, widely documented.
- Hecate (3.5 / parts of 4.1) is omitted — those scenes are commonly regarded as
  non-Shakespearean interpolations and are routinely cut; nothing load-bearing is lost.
