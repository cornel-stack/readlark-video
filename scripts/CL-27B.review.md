## Plot accuracy notes
- Frame: 20 years since Odysseus left Ithaca for Troy (10 war + 10 wandering); presumed dead; 100+ suitors devour the palace pressing Penelope to remarry; her shroud trick (unpicked nightly, betrayed); Telemachus roused by Athena, sails to Nestor & Menelaus for news: correct.
- Odysseus 7 years on Calypso's isle (offered immortality, weeps for home); gods order his release; Poseidon wrecks his raft; washed up on Phaeacia; Nausicaa; King Alcinous's hospitality; weeps at the bard's song; tells his tale (flashback): correct.
- Wanderings (as told at Phaeacia): Lotus-eaters (forgetfulness); Cyclops Polyphemus (Poseidon's son) — cave, men eaten, wine, "Noman", blinding with the stake, neighbours fooled, escape under the rams; the fatal boast of his real name → Polyphemus's curse to Poseidon (home late, alone, all men lost); Aeolus's bag of winds opened by the crew; Circe turns men to swine, Odysseus resists with Hermes' herb (moly), a year, she directs him to the dead: correct.
- Underworld: Tiresias's prophecy (spare the Sun's cattle; else home late/alone/troubled house; peace in old age); his mother's ghost (three failed embraces); Achilles' shade ("rather a living servant than king of the dead"): correct.
- Sirens (lashed to mast, crew's ears waxed); Scylla (6 men) & Charybdis (whirlpool); the cattle of Helios — crew eats them while he sleeps, Zeus destroys the ship, all drown but Odysseus; drifts to Calypso (closing the flashback); Phaeacians carry him home to Ithaca asleep: correct.
- Homecoming: Athena disguises him as a beggar; loyal swineherd Eumaeus; secret reunion with Telemachus; endures the suitors' abuse; the old dog Argos recognises him and dies; Penelope talks with the beggar; nurse Eurycleia recognises the boar-scar (sworn to silence); Penelope announces the contest of the bow (string it + shoot through 12 axe-heads): correct.
- The bow: no suitor can string it; the beggar strings it, shoots through the axes, reveals himself; with Telemachus, Eumaeus (and the cowherd) slaughters the suitors; disloyal maids executed: correct.
- Penelope's test (ordering the bed moved); the secret of the bed built around a living, rooted olive tree; she knows him, reunion; Athena lengthens the night; the suitors' families' feud quieted by Athena; peace: correct.

## Judgment calls
- Butler translation uses the name "Ulysses" in its running text (Latin form); the script standardizes on the Greek "Odysseus" (far more familiar now) and flags in the context scene that older/Butler versions use another name — avoids confusing viewers while staying honest about the source.
- Episode compression: the Laestrygonians, Teiresias's fuller prophecy, and some lesser stops are summarized/omitted for a ~36-min retelling; the script signals it is following the most famous episodes, not every one.
- The harshness of the suitor/maid slaughter is presented honestly (not softened) with brief framing of the older justice-ethic — appropriate for the educational register.
- Thematic emphasis on nostos/homecoming, loyalty-testing, temptation-to-stay (Lotus/Circe/Calypso/Phaeacia), and Penelope as Odysseus's equal — all well-supported by the text.
