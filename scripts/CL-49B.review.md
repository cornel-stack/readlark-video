## Translation / public-domain note
Gutenberg #5200 is the David Wyllie translation, released into the public domain (US). Kafka's
German original (*Die Verwandlung*, 1915) is PD worldwide (Kafka d. 1924). Quoting verbatim is
therefore within hard-rule limits for a public-domain text.

## Judgment calls
- The transformation is deliberately left unexplained (as in the text); the retelling does not
  invent a cause.
- "Vermin / monstrous insect" used rather than a specific species — Kafka's German *Ungeziefer*
  is deliberately unspecific, and he forbade depicting the creature (noted in `close-kafka`).
- Biographical frame (Prague, 1912 composition, the insurance job, the domineering father, the
  instruction never to draw the insect) is standard, widely documented Kafka scholarship.
- The father's arc (broken dependant → uniformed authority) is drawn directly from the text's
  three stages; presented as interpretation in `close-father`.
