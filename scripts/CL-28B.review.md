## Translation / public-domain note
Pope's Iliad (completed 1720) is long out of copyright worldwide. Quoting verbatim is within the
hard-rule limits for a public-domain text. Prose paraphrase throughout the narration follows the
standard events of the poem; only the five cards above are quoted.

## Judgment calls
- Structured as the "wrath arc" (quarrel → ransom), the poem's own frame, not a book-by-book march.
- Achilles' own death (by Paris/Apollo) is **foreshadowed, not depicted** — correct: it falls outside
  the poem, which ends with Hector's funeral. Flagged in scene `c-fate`.
- Hecate/athetised passages n/a (Homer). Hera's manipulation of Zeus, minor duels, and the full
  catalogue of ships are compressed, not altered.
- Number of years: war is in its tenth year; the poem covers ~7 weeks within it.
